Our Story

From dorm room side projects to a growing software agency—here's how two college students turned their passion into a business.

Founded by Passion

Est. December 2023

Ed-Astra started in December 2023 when two college friends realized they shared more than just classes—they shared a vision. While juggling assignments and exams, we spent late nights building apps, experimenting with new frameworks, and dreaming about creating something meaningful.

By November 2024, what began as side projects evolved into something real. Our first client took a chance on us, and we delivered an enterprise ERP system that exceeded expectations. That success gave us the confidence to dream bigger.

In November 2025, we made the boldest decision of our lives: going full-time. We traded the safety of traditional career paths for the excitement of building our own company. Today, Ed-Astra is scaling, our client base is growing, and we're proof that age is just a number when you combine skill, dedication, and hunger to succeed.

We're not just building software. We're building a legacy—one line of code at a time.
